Dear Prof. Koenderink,

I'm working on a small research project applying Koopman-operator /
dynamic-mode-decomposition methods, extended with a Mori-Zwanzig memory
kernel, to subdiffusive particle transport in extracellular-matrix-like
hydrogels. Your 2019 paper, "Particle diffusion in extracellular hydrogels,"
is the closest published match to the system I'm modeling, and I've been
using it as a validation target.

Since raw trajectory data isn't included in the supplement, I digitized
Figure 4a and Supplementary Figure S14a directly from the published PDF to
test whether my method recovers the caging signature you report for the
hyaluronan-collagen composite network. The digitized S14a MSD curve doesn't
extend far enough in Δt to show a statistically significant slope decrease
within my propagated digitization uncertainty, even though the digitized
Fig. 4a ISF panel does show the expected qualitative plateau for the
composite network relative to the pure networks.

Would you be willing to share the raw single-particle trajectory data (or a
longer-range MSD dataset) behind Figures 4a/S14a? It would let me test the
caging signature directly against real data rather than through digitized
summary curves, and I'd be glad to share what I find.

Thank you for considering this, and for the paper itself. It's been a very
useful anchor.
